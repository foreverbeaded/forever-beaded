# Etsy Feedback Workflow Draft

Status: Owner review required. Do not publish or send automatically.

Policy basis reviewed October 31, 2026:

- Etsy Seller Policy: https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers
- Etsy Help, How to Send Messages to Buyers: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015654988-How-to-Send-Messages-to-Buyers
- Etsy Seller Handbook, privacy and express marketing consent: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/59160674124

Policy uncertainty: Etsy's current official material does not explicitly confirm that a seller may place off-Etsy website testimonials in the Etsy About section. The draft below is accurately sourced and permission-gated, but the owner should obtain confirmation from Etsy Support before publishing it.

## Policy Guardrails

- Etsy reviews remain inside Etsy's official review system. Website feedback cannot be imported as an Etsy review or represented as Etsy-verified.
- A website review is not cleared for Etsy or other marketing use unless the customer separately gives express written permission.
- Any reused website testimonial must keep the customer's approved display name and exact wording.
- Every reused website testimonial must carry this source label: **Forever Beaded customer feedback — website purchase**.
- Do not include website star ratings in Etsy testimonial copy.
- Do not include customer email, order details, delivery details, or admin metadata.
- Review requests must ask for honest feedback, never a five-star rating, and must not offer a conditional incentive.
- Send at most one individual follow-up for a genuine delivered Etsy order. Do not automate it and do not message a buyer who has opted out or already reviewed.

## Website Permission Workflow

1. In the private review dashboard, open **Approved** reviews only.
2. Exclude owner tests and any review whose authenticity is uncertain.
3. Ask the customer separately for written permission to reuse their exact website feedback in external marketing. Website publication consent alone is not enough.
4. Record the permission state as requested, granted, declined, or revoked, including where the written response was received.
5. Export only approved reviews marked **granted**.
6. Before any Etsy use, verify the exported wording and display name against the stored review and retain the source label.
7. If permission is revoked or the website review is removed, stop using it externally and mark the record **revoked**.

The private Etsy preparation queue synchronizes automatically when a review is approved, rejected, removed, exported, or its permission changes:

- **Awaiting permission**: approved on the website, but separate external-marketing permission is missing.
- **Ready**: approved and separately permission-granted; available to copy or export.
- **Exported**: included in a private owner export. This does not mean it was published on Etsy.
- **Excluded**: rejected, removed, declined, or revoked and never awaiting external cleanup.
- **Action required**: previously exported wording must be manually corrected or removed because the review or permission later changed.

Suggested permission request:

> Thank you again for sharing your feedback with Forever Beaded. Would you be comfortable with us reusing your exact review and approved display name in Forever Beaded marketing, including a clearly labelled website-purchase testimonial in our Etsy shop? This is optional, and saying no will not affect your order or review. We would not include your email, order details, or an Etsy star rating. You can change your mind later by contacting us.

## Etsy Review Request Templates

### Message to Buyers

Thank you for supporting Forever Beaded and handmade work. Once Etsy makes your order eligible for a review, you are welcome to share your honest experience through Etsy's Purchases and Reviews area. Thoughtful feedback of any rating helps us learn and helps future Etsy shoppers. If anything needs attention, please contact us through Etsy Messages so we can help.

### Optional One-Time Post-Delivery Follow-Up

Hi [first name],

I hope your Forever Beaded order arrived safely and that you are enjoying it. If Etsy shows the option and you have a moment, I would be grateful for your honest feedback through Etsy's official review area. There is no pressure, and no need to reply if you have already reviewed your order.

If anything about your order needs attention, please reply here through Etsy Messages and I will be happy to help.

Thank you for supporting handmade,
Forever Beaded

## Private Tracking Checklist

Keep the populated checklist in a private owner-controlled location, not in this public repository. Use the header-only template in `etsy/etsy-review-tracking-template.csv`.

| Field | Allowed value / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Etsy order reference | Etsy's order reference only; do not duplicate the buyer's name, email, or address |
| Delivery status | Not confirmed / Confirmed delivered |
| Review eligibility | Not open / Eligible / Closed / Unknown |
| Review-request status | Not applicable / Not sent / Sent once / Buyer opted out |
| Official Etsy review received | Yes / No / Unknown |
| Website testimonial reuse permission | Not requested / Requested / Granted / Declined / Revoked |
| Last checked | Date only |
| Private note | Optional, minimal operational note with no unnecessary personal information |

Do not add a website testimonial to Etsy until its separate permission status is **granted**. Do not add stars, an Etsy buyer badge, or wording that suggests an Etsy-verified purchase.

## Owner Release Checklist

- Confirm Etsy Support permits clearly sourced, permission-approved website testimonials in the About section.
- Review every exported testimonial for a granted permission record.
- Confirm no test, pending, rejected, declined, or revoked review is included.
- Confirm exact wording and display name are unchanged.
- Confirm the website-purchase source label is visible.
- Confirm no stars, email, order information, or private metadata appears.
- Approve the final About copy and any review-request template before manual use.
